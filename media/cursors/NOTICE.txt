This is original authored work (G7 clean-room). The cursors were drawn from
scratch for TK Reawakened and are not traced or derived from any legacy
binary, game, screenshot, or fetched fixture.

Both cursors are 32×32 pixels, complying with the round-7 cursor rule
(docs/spec/engine/rpgcode-functions.md#reawakened-default-cursors).
`tkr-arrow.png` is the default mouse pointer with hotspot at (0, 0).
`tkr-hand.png` is the default cursor-map hand rendered at (mapX - 40, mapY - 10).
