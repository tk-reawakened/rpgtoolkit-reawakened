# A scene builds its board, with vectors made from tile and asset collision

## Scope

Implements the lowering of a scene to a legacy `.brd` in `editor-model`, through the board writer `core` already has: tiles and their lookup table, placed pack assets expanded into images or tiles, and the board's solid and under vectors built from the collision stored with each tile and each asset. The build is repeatable, never reorders existing slots, and records the hash of what it wrote so an outside edit to the `.brd` is found and offered as an import, not overwritten.

Follow [ADR 0019](../../../docs/adr/0019-tkr-authoring-formats.md) decision 4 and consequences 2 and 3, and section 5 of the draft [`docs/spec/formats/tkr-authoring.md`](../../../docs/spec/formats/tkr-authoring.md) as replaced by the contract pages of 27.8, 27.11 and 27.12. The documents obey the rules in [`docs/spec/formats/native-contracts.md`](../../../docs/spec/formats/native-contracts.md) (common stamp, identity by path, closed top level, `pluginData`), and the shared editor contracts in [ADR 0022](../../../docs/adr/0022-shared-editor-authoring-contracts.md). Nothing converts on open or on save ([ADR 0019](../../../docs/adr/0019-tkr-authoring-formats.md) decision 5): the build is a function the caller invokes.

## Acceptance and verification

On an authored scene: two builds give identical bytes; the built board loads in the headless runner and its draw list matches the scene's own preview; a tile marked solid yields a solid vector over its cells and a pack asset's under vector appears moved to its placement; adding one object leaves every earlier slot index unchanged; a byte changed in the built `.brd` is reported before the next build writes.

Preserve same-version unknown data through the build, and assert the repeatability and slot-order boundaries directly rather than replaying an authoring session. Follow `discovery/inception-proposal.md`, G2 (the YAML reader stays out of `core`), G5 (the runner's inputs are ordinary boards) and G7; do not claim checks passed without executing them.

## Dependencies and decision gate

Prerequisites: 12.1, 27.13, 27.14. Follow the reader and writer behaviour of 27.13 and 27.14 and the contract pages written by 27.8, 27.11 and 27.12; the tileset and asset-pack readers are the only source of the collision the build lowers into vectors.

## Implementation sequence

1. In `packages/editor-model`, lower validated scene records into the public core `Board` model. Use the inverse field table in [native-scene.md](../../../docs/spec/formats/native-scene.md#disposition-of-every-board-field) as the mapping, and use `packages/native-data-import/src/scene.ts` and its authored fixtures as the independent round-trip check. Resolve floor IDs to legacy layer numbers without shifting empty palette, program, item or image slots. Preserve tile and shading residue and the existing code-page refusal.
2. Resolve declared tile and pack dependencies with `packages/native-data`, then expand only what the build can encode. Validate output bounds and path ownership before writing any artifacts. Append generated vectors after authored vectors and keep the source document unchanged. A placed fence is the acceptance proof for separate solid and under polygons: tile-owned geometry translates with each placement; the player stops at the rail and can occupy the clear portion.
3. Define the compiler's artifact inventory before host integration: output path, generated bytes, input revision and notes/refusals. Keep mutation and freshness handling on the editor/platform-services side. Check the actual on-disk board against the last successful build hash before writing, and keep externally changed bytes untouched until the explicit recovery path is accepted.
4. Verify the round trip through the production board reader, compare independent expected vectors and visible output in the headless runner, then run the external-edit refusal and stable-slot case. The existing legacy run path alone is not scene build evidence.

The painted pond also depends on the native terrain resolver and authoring path ([#1733](https://github.com/tk-reawakened/rpgtoolkit-reawakened/issues/1733), [#1851](https://github.com/tk-reawakened/rpgtoolkit-reawakened/issues/1851)). This story provides the compiler boundary; the editor acceptance must drive the same compiler rather than a preview-only resolver. The scene and asset-pack sections are already definitional, but the [terrain cutover rules](../../../docs/design/terrain-scene-formats.md#approved-cutover-decisions) supersede the older tileset geometry and allocation assumptions. Specify the flat build rules before writing the geometry and art-mapping code; in particular distinguish explicit solid/under shapes from optional `boundarySources` contour tracing. Do not infer raised-block projection or promote unsupported rendering behavior.
